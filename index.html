MY JOURNEY

My Story: Learning, Building and Growing

My journey into technology began with curiosity, determination and a desire to solve real-world problems.

Where My Journey Began

I attended Ndanai Primary School before joining Boito Boys for my secondary education. During secondary school, I was supported by the Equity Elimu Scholarship, which helped me continue my education.

Computer Studies became one of my favourite subjects. I enjoyed learning how computers work, helping fellow students understand computer topics and participating in computer-related projects.

Those experiences encouraged me to pursue technology beyond the classroom.

From Cyber Café Work to Software Development

During school holidays, I gained practical experience working at Beetech Cyber and later SkyNet Cyber. These opportunities exposed me to customer service, computer use, online services and everyday technology challenges.

After moving to Sotik, I continued working on projects while facing financial challenges that made college difficult to pursue at the time.

Instead of giving up on my goal, I chose self-directed learning. I began developing my programming skills and building real projects that could demonstrate what I was learning.

Building Solutions for Real Businesses

I developed a website for PrimeNest Company and started building KIVARO, a business management platform designed to help businesses manage their operations digitally.

These projects continue to help me learn software architecture, frontend development, backend APIs, databases, deployment and the practical work involved in maintaining software.

My Vision

I want to become a skilled software engineer, develop reliable digital products and help businesses use technology effectively.

I am also looking for legitimate educational opportunities, scholarships or sponsorship that could help me pursue formal studies in Software Engineering or Information Technology.

I believe that consistent learning, practical experience and persistence can create opportunities for the future.
